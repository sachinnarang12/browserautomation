Executive Summary

AutomatePortal is an AI-powered browser automation platform that enables businesses to automate repetitive web portal tasks — downloading reports, submitting forms, extracting data — without writing code. The platform uses natural language instructions powered by Nova Act AI, making it accessible to non-technical users.

Competitive Advantage

AI-First Approach

Unlike traditional RPA tools (UiPath, Automation Anywhere) that require recording macros or visual scripting, AutomatePortal uses natural language instructions. Users describe what they want in plain English.

SMB-Friendly Pricing

Enterprise RPA tools cost $5,000-50,000/year. AutomatePortal starts at $29/month, making automation accessible to small businesses and individual professionals.

Key SaaS Metrics to Track

MRR / ARR

Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue. The north star metric for subscription health.

Churn Rate

% of customers leaving per month. Target < 5% for SMB SaaS.

LTV

Lifetime Value. Average revenue per customer over their lifetime.

CAC

Customer Acquisition Cost. Marketing + sales spend to acquire one customer.

NRR

Net Revenue Retention. Revenue from existing customers (upgrades - churn).

Time to Value

Minutes from signup to first successful automation. Target < 15 minutes.

Legal & Compliance Checklist

Required Before Launch

  • Terms of Service
  • Privacy Policy (GDPR + CCPA compliant)
  • Cookie Consent Banner
  • Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
  • Business Entity Registration (LLC/Corp)
  • Business Insurance (E&O / Cyber)

Security & Compliance

  • SOC 2 Type I (within 6 months of launch)
  • Data encryption at rest and in transit
  • Regular security penetration testing
  • Incident response plan
  • Data retention and deletion policies
  • Right to data portability (GDPR Art. 20)
Important: Since AutomatePortal stores customer credentials to third-party portals, security is paramount. Credential isolation via OS keyring (or AWS Secrets Manager in production) is a key differentiator and must be maintained. Consider SOC 2 compliance early to unlock enterprise deals.
